# Common area buttons

The state / area step retains the IANA tzdb regions and US state mappings, then adds up to eight populous cities per country plus national capitals. The Philippines uses a short curated list of familiar urban areas. Countries and territories with fewer places may have fewer options. Cities with the same timezone remain separate area buttons.

Common city names and IANA timezone mappings are derived from [GeoNames cities15000](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip), retrieved 2026-10-18. Population ranking chooses the shortlist; selected Egyptian names use common English spellings. GeoNames data is provided under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribution: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/). The country and timezone region tables remain from the public-domain IANA tz database.

Runtime offsets are computed by Intl for the scheduled week, rather than stored in the city data. Adding a city does not create a distinct timezone or collect phone area codes.
